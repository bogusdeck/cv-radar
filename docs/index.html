Why CV-RADAR?

🎯

ATS Scoring Engine

Go-powered engine evaluating exact keyword match, keyword density, role fit, and penalty conditions across major ATS systems.

🔍

Missing Keyword Audit

Automatically scans job descriptions and pinpoints every critical skill, tool, and qualification omitted from your resume.

🖥️

Terminal UI Dashboard

Interactive cv-tui dashboard built with Bubble Tea for live platform scoring, grade calculations (A–F), and recommendations.

🤖

Universal AI Agent Skill

Registered skill for Antigravity, Claude Code, Codex, and OpenCode. Just type /cv-optimizer in chat.

Background execution script (optimize.sh) that orchestrates LLMs to tailor your CV content without manual copy-pasting.

Supported ATS Platforms

Platform Scoring Strategy Key Focus
Workday Strict exact-keyword matching Requires precise terminology from the JD. Heavy penalties for missing skills.
Taleo Keyword density scoring Boosts scores when critical JD keywords are repeated across summary and experience points.
Greenhouse Semantic AI + human reviewer fit Focuses on natural phrasing, contextual impact, and measurable achievements.
iCIMS Mixed exact + semantic match Primary weight on job title alignment, structural cleanliness, and date formats.
